I finally got a 3D printer, and of course I couldn't leave it alone — this video covers unboxing and setting up a QIDI Q2, printing the first Benchy, and turning it into a proper "homelab 3D printer": an air-gapped network with local DNS/NTP and a trusted SSL cert, Home Assistant integration with a local voice pipeline, Spoolman filament tracking backed by a custom sync tool, and OrcaSlicer tweaks so the printer auto-selects the right filament.

If you're into homelab, self-hosting, or just want a printer that isn't phoning out to "the cloud," this one's for you.
